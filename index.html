Delete your MaxMovies account

You can delete your MaxMovies account yourself, from inside the app, at any time. You do not need to ask anyone, and you do not need to give a reason.

In the app

  1. Open MaxMovies and go to the Library tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It cannot be undone, and the account cannot be recovered afterwards.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, email address, display name and password hash.
  • Your favourites, watchlist and liked titles.
  • Your ratings.
  • Your viewing history and progress.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing that identifies you is kept. Server logs recorded for security and abuse prevention may still contain the IP address a request came from; they are retained only as long as needed for that purpose and then deleted or anonymised. No backup copy of a deleted account is retained beyond 30 days.

If you cannot use the app

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, write to mowiefour@proton.me from the email address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
